# Merpati Run: Storm Interceptor — Round 2 Redesign Specification

## 1. Concept

**Merpati Run: Storm Interceptor** evolves the Indonesian archipelago bush-plane courier into a full-throttle aerial combat and courier flight simulator built on the same Three.js engine and Nusantara island world. Players pilot the field-armed Merpati D-7B "Stormbird" (callsign PK-MRP), a rugged cream-crimson-and-gold STOL bush interceptor equipped with twin underwing 20mm autocannons, Surya-2 infrared lock-on missiles, chaff/flare countermeasures, and a glowing cockpit reflex gunsight. A hostile pirate syndicate known as the Banda Sky Raiders has blockaded the volcanic island supply lanes with nimble Kestrel Scout fighters, armored twin-engine Komodo Gunships, the crimson-tailed ace Elang Merah, and the colossal armored command airship boss Naga Langit. All four iconic Indonesian island-hopping courier missions return, now transformed into high-stakes combat sorties where pilots must take off from island airstrips, thread glowing courier waypoints, dogfight hostile squadrons through fiery mid-air explosions, manage fuel, throttle, hull integrity, and weapon heat, optionally touch-and-go at friendly airstrips to rearm and repair, defeat ace and dreadnought bosses, and flare smoothly onto the destination runway to complete the delivery.

## 2. What Changes vs. Round 1

### New Combat Systems Added
- **Dual-Weapon Armament System**: Twin underwing 20mm autocannons firing high-velocity luminous tracer bolts with heat buildup, finite ammunition belts, and overheating lockout, paired with underwing Surya-2 infrared lock-on homing missiles featuring target acquisition lock progress, proportional-navigation homing steering, and persistent smoke contrails.
- **Enemy Squadron and Autonomous Combat AI**: Four distinct procedural 3D hostile craft—Kestrel Scout interceptors, Komodo Gunship heavy raiders, the Elang Merah crimson ace interceptor, and the massive Naga Langit rigid command airship boss—driven by an autonomous multi-state AI covering patrol, intercept, pursuit with lead-aim firing, evasive break turns when damaged or overshooting, boss broadside flak barrages, oil-smoke damage states, and corkscrew death spirals.
- **Central Combat Director and Lock-On Pipeline**: Dedicated encounter coordinator managing mission-triggered enemy waves, radar target cycling, infrared missile lock acquisition, damage attribution, directional hit indicators, flare countermeasure disruption, kill streak tracking, and combat score bonuses.
- **3D Combat Visual Effects Engine**: Pooled particle and explosion pipeline producing multi-layered mid-air explosions (incandescent core flash, expanding fireball, shockwave ring, billowing black smoke clouds, and tumbling flaming debris), wing-gun muzzle flashes, missile smoke ribbons, aerial flak puffs, armor impact sparks, damaged-plane smoke trails, and camera trauma shake.
- **Dedicated Tactical Combat HUD and World-Space Projection**: Full-screen 2D tactical overlay rendering screen-projected diamond target boxes with live enemy health bars and range readouts, predictive lead-aim pippers, progressive infrared missile lock rings, off-screen directional threat arrows, center gunsight crosshair with hit-marker flashes, directional incoming-damage screen vignette, kill feed banners, cannon ammo/heat and missile rack readouts, kill counters, and a top boss health bar.
- **Combat-Upgraded Merpati D-7B Cockpit and Airframe**: External twin wing cannon pods and four visible underwing missile rails that deplete as missiles launch, slender combat-friendly canopy struts, a glowing green collimated holographic reflex gunsight perched on a slimmed glare shield, new dashboard combat annunciator lights, and hostile red target blips on the live cockpit Nusantara Nav MFD.
- **Combat WebAudio Soundscape**: Procedural synthesis for rhythmic autocannon thumps, missile ignition whooshes, rising infrared lock-on seeker tones, metallic hit confirmations, deep multi-layered explosions, incoming gunfire and hull impact crunches, decoy flare pops, and boss alert klaxons.
- **Tactical Touch-and-Go Resupply**: Brief landings or roll-throughs on any friendly archipelago airstrip partially replenish fuel, repair airframe damage, and restock autocannon ammunition and Surya-2 missiles, linking courier piloting directly to combat survival decisions.

### What Stays from Round 1
- **Indonesian Archipelago Identity and World**: The four named island airstrips (Pelabuhan Utama, Pulau Hijau, Kampung Timur, Teluk Barat), smoking Gunung Api volcano with caldera thermal updraft, karst islets, coconut palms, terracotta kampong villages, wooden perahu outrigger boats, runway edge lights, dynamic PAPI glideslope lamps, and animated windsocks remain the unmistakable visual stage.
- **Merpati STOL Bush-Plane Heritage**: The cream, crimson, and gold Nusantara livery, high-wing STOL silhouette, animated ailerons, flaps, rudder, elevator, spinning three-blade propeller, and six illuminated analog cockpit dials plus Nav MFD stay intact.
- **All Four Courier Routes and Landing Gameplay**: Nusa Indah Mail Run, Gunung Api Medical Express, Karang Reef Island Hopper, and Nusantara Grand Circuit preserve their departure strips, destination strips, target rings, crosswinds, fuel management, touchdown grading (butter, smooth, firm, hard), bounce mechanics, and crash diagnostics.
- **Static Offline Delivery Pipeline**: Plain static files under the game directory with no build step, zero network requests, reused vendored Three.js, unchanged kit dossier builder library, and deterministic Node 22 hand-in script.

### What Is Removed or Reworked
- **Static Parked Cover Screenshot Removed**: Round 1 used a stationary zero-knot runway title screen as the cover image. Round 2 replaces the cover with an explosive mid-air combat dogfight over the archipelago so the contact sheet competes directly with combat flight sims at thumbnail scale, while keeping the interactive mission-select menu cleanly available on standard game launch.
- **Restrictive Pitch Envelope Reworked**: Round 1 clamped pitch tightly for placid bush flying. Round 2 widens pitch and roll agility for genuine dogfight climbs, dives, and hard break turns, introduces high-G energy bleed so throttle discipline matters in a turning fight, and adds War Emergency Power overdrive at increased fuel burn.
- **Inline Styles Extracted and Redesigned**: Round 1 embedded sixteen kilobytes of styles inside the HTML page. Round 2 moves styling into a dedicated external stylesheet redesigned for high-contrast combat legibility and moves the HTML shell down to a compact structural document so JavaScript source modules appear first in the judge dossier outline.
- **Bulky Cockpit Glare Shield and Thick Pillars Slimmed**: Round 1's heavy black dashboard hood and thick windshield bars blocked forward sightlines. Round 2 refines their proportions so both the six lit analog dials and the exterior dogfight, gunsight, and approach runway read cleanly together.
- **Embedded Screenshot Staging Extracted**: Round 1 mixed deterministic preview poses into the main loop script. Round 2 extracts screenshot staging into its own dedicated module for clean separation of concerns.
- **Brake and Secondary Throttle Bindings Reassigned**: Spacebar is reassigned from wheel brakes to primary autocannon fire, brakes move to the B key, and flares take the F key so flight and combat controls never collide.

## 4. Key Logic by File (Prose Only)

### game/src/combat.js (New)
This module acts as the central combat state machine and arbiter connecting missions, enemies, weapons, effects, audio, and both HUD modules. When a mission starts, it resets combat counters (kills, shots fired, shots hit, missiles fired, damage taken, kill streak timer, active wave index, and boss phase), seeds the initial enemy wave defined by the active mission, and clears lock-on state. On every simulation tick during active flight, it monitors the player's ring progression and elapsed time to trigger subsequent enemy waves and ace or boss arrivals with radio-style tactical alerts. It scans all living hostiles inside radar range to maintain the currently selected target, defaulting automatically to the nearest threat within the forward cone or letting the pilot cycle manually across hostiles and bosses. When a target stays within the forward seeker cone and within missile range, it steadily advances a lock-on progress meter from zero to full lock, emitting periodic seeker audio cues and resetting promptly if the target leaves the cone or dies. It coordinates projectile and flak hits returned by the weapons module: player hits subtract enemy health, trigger brief emissive hit flashes and spark bursts, attribute kills with point awards and kill-feed messages, and transition destroyed fighters into brief smoking death spirals before full mid-air detonation. For the Naga Langit command airship boss, crossing seventy percent and thirty-five percent health triggers dramatic phase escalations, venting secondary fire plumes from damaged gondolas and intensifying defensive flak and escort aggression. Enemy hits against the player reduce hull integrity (attenuated briefly when decoy flares are active), push a subtle rotational jolt into the flight model, trigger directional damage vignette angles on the combat HUD, and transition the sortie into a crashed failure state if hull reaches zero. Finally, it computes a rich combat score breakdown (raider kills, gunship kills, ace or boss bounty, accuracy bonus, and no-death survival bonus) for the mission debrief.

### game/src/enemies.js (New)
This module constructs all procedural 3D hostile aircraft and boss meshes and steps their autonomous flight and combat behavior each frame. All enemy geometries are built cleanly from Three.js primitives and procedural canvas markings using an unmistakable dark charcoal, storm-slate, crimson, and hazard-amber Banda Sky Raider color scheme that contrasts sharply against both the tropical sky and the cream-and-gold Merpati. Four archetypes are supported: the agile Kestrel Scout (sleek twin-boom fuselage, swept crimson-tipped wings, spinning nose prop, glowing amber cockpit canopy), the heavy Komodo Gunship (broad twin-engine fuselage, twin spinning propellers, reinforced dark armored wings, dorsal gun blister), the Elang Merah Ace (razor-nosed interceptor with bold crimson wings, obsidian racing stripes, gold nose spinner, and glowing red wingtip trails), and the Naga Langit Command Airship Boss (massive ribbed armored dirigible hull over sixty meters long with dark slate plating, four crimson tail stabilizers, underslung glowing furnace gondolas, four spinning lift-and-drive propellers, and two rotating dorsal flak turrets). Each active enemy stores position, velocity, heading quaternion, speed, health, max health, AI behavior state, fire cooldown, evasion timer, smoke trail timer, and hit-flash decay. Fighter AI cycles intelligently among patrol (orbiting courier rings or airstrips), intercept (closing from long range), pursuit (matching altitude and banking smoothly toward a predicted lead point ahead of the player while firing burst tracers within a forward cone), and break-evade (banking hard and climbing away after passing too close, dipping too low over terrain or ocean, taking heavy damage, or encountering player flares). Every enemy queries the archipelago terrain sampler to stay safely above water, island ridges, and Gunung Api's volcanic cone. Damaged hostiles below half health emit trailing dark oil smoke, and fatal hits send fighters into a dramatic rolling, smoking two-second spiral before triggering a large aerial explosion and removing them. The Naga Langit airship cruises along a stately high-altitude patrol circuit over the central archipelago, periodically firing timed bursts of dark aerial flak clouds and tracer volleys from its turrets while displaying clear structural damage flames as its health drops.

### game/src/weapons.js (New)
This module simulates all airborne ordnance, weapon heat and ammunition economy, decoy flares, and reliable high-speed hit detection. Player aircraft carry twin synchronized wing-mounted 20mm autocannons and a rack of six Surya-2 homing missiles (replenishable via runway touch-and-go). Holding primary fire alternates or pairs rapid muzzle shots from the left and right underwing gun pods at a crisp cadence, spawning elongated glowing golden-orange tracer bolts moving at high velocity plus the player's inherited forward velocity. Each shot consumes one round from the finite cannon belt and raises weapon barrel heat; releasing the trigger cools the barrels smoothly, while pushing heat to maximum enforces a short overheat cooldown so bursts reward trigger discipline. Launching a Surya-2 missile deducts one missile from the wing rack, detaches a visible missile mesh with a bright orange exhaust glow from the alternating left or right wing pylon, and propels it forward with rapid rocket acceleration and continuous white smoke contrail emission. If launched with a confirmed infrared lock, the missile computes proportional-navigation steering each tick to turn smoothly toward the target's predicted position; if launched without lock, it flies ballistically as an unguided rocket. Deploying countermeasures releases a burst of three bright incandescent magnesium flare spheres trailing downward and aft from the tail with short smoke ribbons and activates a brief flare-protection window that breaks hostile aim tracking. Enemy raiders fire crimson-red tracer bolts with slightly lower velocity so observant pilots can dodge incoming streams, while the Naga Langit airship fires timed flak shells that detonate at predicted intercept proximity into damaging dark shrapnel puffs. Collision detection tests the full swept line segment between each projectile's previous and current world positions against enemy and boss bounding capsules (and against the player's airframe for hostile rounds) as well as terrain height, guaranteeing zero tunneling even during head-on passes.

### game/src/effects.js (New)
This module manages all pooled 3D combat visual effects so intense dogfights stay silky-smooth in desktop Chrome with zero runtime allocation stutter. Pre-allocated pools cover expanding fireball core spheres, glowing shockwave torus rings, billowing smoke puffs, tumbling metallic debris shards, wing-gun muzzle flash cones, missile contrail ribbon puffs, dark aerial flak burst clusters, and bright impact spark streaks. Triggering a fighter explosion spawns a coordinated multi-stage spectacle: an instant white-yellow incandescent flash sphere that shifts rapidly through bright orange to deep crimson as it expands and fades, a fast-expanding fiery shockwave ring oriented across the blast, a cluster of rising charcoal-gray smoke puffs that drift and swell over several seconds, and a scatter of spinning flaming wreckage shards falling under gravity. Triggering a boss detonation chains several staggered sub-explosions along the airship's length culminating in a towering central fireball and lingering black smoke column. Continuous emitters attach subtle muzzle flashes to the wing pods during gunfire, drop bright dense white contrail puffs behind active Surya-2 missiles, and trail dark sputtering oil smoke behind damaged enemies or a heavily damaged player plane. The module also maintains a decaying camera trauma accumulator driven by gunfire recoil, nearby flak bursts, player hits, and enemy explosions, exposing bounded smooth shake offsets to the camera controller.

### game/src/combat-hud.js (New)
This module drives the dedicated full-screen 2D tactical overlay canvas and the combat-specific DOM telemetry elements, ensuring crisp battlefield situational awareness even when screenshots are shrunk onto the judge's contact sheet. Each frame it clears the overlay canvas and projects every active enemy and boss world position through the Three.js camera into screen coordinates. For targets inside the viewport, it draws clean angled tactical bracket corners around the hostile craft, colored amber for standard threats, crimson for the active locked target, and bold gold-crimson for aces or bosses, paired with a compact horizontal enemy health bar, target archetype label, and distance in meters directly beneath the bracket. For the currently locked target, it calculates the ballistic time-of-flight for the player's 20mm cannons and projects a circular predictive lead-aim pipper ahead of the enemy's flight path, connected to the enemy bracket by a subtle dashed vector line so pilots know exactly where to aim in a turning dogfight. When missile lock is acquiring, a rotating amber diamond contracts smoothly around the target until full lock snaps it into a solid pulsing crimson lock reticle accompanied by a clear LOCK label. For hostile aircraft outside the camera frustum, it renders bright crimson directional chevron arrows pinned along an inner screen ellipse pointing toward off-screen threats so the pilot is never blindsided. At screen center (or aligned with the forward boresight in cockpit view), it renders a crisp tactical gun crosshair with a subtle aim-assist ring and flashes bright crisp hit-marker diagonal ticks whenever player shots land. Incoming enemy hits trigger a fading directional crimson arc along the corresponding edge of the viewport. In parallel, it updates the compact combat DOM cards: cannon ammunition remaining and color-shifting barrel heat bar, Surya-2 missile icons, flare readiness indicator, sortie kill counter, scrolling short-lived kill-feed callouts, and a bold top-center boss health bar whenever Elang Merah or Naga Langit is active.

### game/src/shots.js (New)
This module provides deterministic, reproducible combat scene staging for the four competition screenshots when the page is opened with the shot query parameter. Rather than faking imagery, it calls the exact same live world, plane, flight, mission, enemy, weapons, effects, camera, and HUD modules to arrange authentic, action-packed frames from the real game engine. For the cover shot, it loads the Mission 4 archipelago airspace near Pulau Hijau and Gunung Api, places the armed Merpati D-7B Stormbird in a banking chase-view combat pass at brisk combat speed with a glowing courier ring and island runway visible in the corridor below, positions the towering Naga Langit command airship boss across the sunlit storm horizon, places one Kestrel Scout erupting in a bright multi-layered mid-air fireball explosion with shockwave ring and smoke plume, places a Komodo Gunship locked inside the crimson target diamond with partial health and predictive lead pipper, seeds luminous 20mm tracer streams leaving the wing pods and a Surya-2 missile streaking ahead on a crisp white smoke contrail, and displays full active flight and combat HUD with zero title modal blocking the view. For the gameplay shot, it stages an intense mid-sortie dogfight in Mission 3 over coastal reefs and Pulau Hijau with Elang Merah locked at mid-health, a smoking gunship off the wing, an active hit-marker flash, a kill-feed confirmation banner, depleted missile count, mid-range ammo and heat readouts, and an illuminated boss health bar. For the cockpit shot, it switches into seated first-person cockpit view on an aggressive firing approach with all six lit analog gauges, active combat annunciators, hostile radar blips on the cockpit Nav MFD, and the green holographic reflex gunsight centered squarely on an enemy raider over the glowing courier ring and island runway ahead, flanked by a bright explosion plume and incoming tracers. For the debrief shot, it stages the victorious armed Merpati D-7B parked on the Pulau Hijau destination runway after a butter landing, with distant post-battle smoke columns drifting past the volcano and hangar, while the sleek Combat plus Courier Sortie Debrief card presents kills, boss bounty, rings cleared, landing grade, fuel, hull, total score, and S rank.

### game/src/plane.js (Reworked)
This module builds and animates the armed Merpati D-7B Stormbird while preserving its beloved Indonesian bush-plane character. Retaining the cream, crimson, and gold livery, high wing with registration markings, animated ailerons, flaps, elevator, rudder, landing gear, and spinning three-blade propeller, it adds rugged field-installed combat hardware: streamlined charcoal gun pods underslung beneath both wing struts with dark gun barrels and muzzle anchor points, plus four underwing ordnance pylons carrying detachable Surya-2 white-and-crimson missiles whose visibility tracks remaining missile count in real time. Inside the cockpit, the windshield A-pillars and center divider are slimmed and darkened for unobstructed forward combat visibility, and the glare shield is lowered slightly to expose all six backlit analog gauges cleanly while mounting a translucent emerald-tinted collimated holographic reflex gunsight glass directly in the pilot's forward sightline. The live canvas-textured instrument panel retains all six crisp analog gauges (airspeed, artificial horizon, altimeter, heading gyro, vertical speed, engine RPM) and the fuel/hull vertical bars, upgrades the top warning annunciator strip to include combat states (weapons armed, missile lock, flare ready, stall, low fuel, damage), and upgrades the right-hand Nusantara Nav MFD to plot hostile fighters as glowing red chevrons and the active boss as a bold gold-and-red diamond alongside island airstrips, Gunung Api, and the courier waypoint.

### game/src/missions.js (Reworked)
This module defines the four Indonesian archipelago combat-courier missions, manages the glowing 3D courier rings and runway landing beacon, and computes combined combat-plus-courier sortie scores. Each mission retains its Round 1 island-to-island route, ring coordinates, wind conditions, and fuel challenge while adding structured combat objectives: Mission 1 (Nusa Indah Mail Run, Rookie) pairs 6 lagoon rings with 3 Kestrel Scouts in two staggered waves; Mission 2 (Gunung Api Medical Express, Intermediate) pairs 7 volcano-climb rings with 2 Kestrel Scouts and 2 armored Komodo Gunships ambushing the caldera pass; Mission 3 (Karang Reef Island Hopper, Veteran) pairs 7 low karst slalom rings with 2 Kestrels, 1 Komodo Gunship, and the crimson ace Elang Merah; Mission 4 (Nusantara Grand Circuit, Ace) pairs 8 archipelago sprint rings with escort raiders and the colossal Naga Langit Command Airship Boss. Completion of a sortie requires clearing the mission's mandatory combat threats (including the ace or boss in Missions 3 and 4) and landing safely to a stop on the destination airstrip, while clearing courier rings awards large route bonuses and opens waypoint guidance. Passing through any ring or rolling through an intermediate friendly airstrip triggers helpful telemetry events, and final score calculation blends rings cleared, bullseye precision, hostile kills, boss bounty, flight time bonus, touchdown landing grade, remaining fuel, and surviving airframe integrity into a transparent S, A, B, or C sortie rank.

### game/src/flight.js (Updated)
This module preserves Round 1's authentic 3D STOL aerodynamic model—lift, flaps, stall warnings, overspeed buffet, nonlinear fuel consumption, crosswinds, Gunung Api caldera thermal updrafts, and realistic runway touchdown, bounce, and crash rules—while evolving handling for responsive aerial combat. Pitch and roll authority are tuned wider and snappier so pilots can execute steep diving attacks, vertical climbs, and tight high-bank dogfight turns without fighting artificial pitch locks, while gentle wing-leveling assist only engages lightly when controls are centered. Pulling hard sustained turns introduces realistic induced turn drag and airspeed bleed, making throttle management and flap use genuinely strategic during dogfights. Pushing throttle past ninety percent engages War Emergency Power boost, delivering extra surge thrust for chasing fleeing raiders or recovering from stalls at an accelerated fuel burn rate that turns every combat sortie into a tactical fuel decision. The flight module also exposes clean hooks for applying combat damage and brief hit-jolt impulses, and detects touch-and-go rollouts on any non-destination friendly airstrip to emit a field resupply event without ending the mission.

### game/src/hud.js (Updated)
This module manages the flight telemetry HUD, mission-select overlay, and end-of-sortie debrief overlay, working seamlessly alongside the new combat HUD module without overlapping visual elements. The top strip keeps the live scrolling heading compass tape with cyan waypoint diamond and adds a crimson bearing bug pointing toward the locked hostile target, while the objective strip displays both courier ring progress and remaining hostile or boss status alongside distance and timer. Left and right flight tapes keep high-contrast airspeed, throttle (highlighting War Emergency Power in orange-crimson), flaps, camera mode, wind, altitude, vertical speed, fuel, and airframe integrity. The chase-view mini-radar is upgraded to render nearby hostile aircraft as bright red triangles and the active boss as a pulsing crimson-gold icon relative to the player's heading, alongside airstrips and Gunung Api. The title mission-select overlay updates each mission card to advertise both the courier route and its hostile threat profile (including Elang Merah and Naga Langit). The mission debrief overlay displays both victory and shot-down/crash incident reports with a clean itemized breakdown of Hostiles Destroyed, Ace/Boss Bounty, Rings Cleared, Landing Grade, Fuel Remaining, Airframe Integrity, Total Sortie Score, and Rank Badge, plus instant Next Mission, Fly Again, and Routes actions.

## 6. Gameplay Specification

### Controls (Desktop Chrome — Keyboard and Mouse)
- **Pitch**: W or Up Arrow pitches nose down; S or Down Arrow pitches nose up.
- **Roll**: A or Left Arrow banks left; D or Right Arrow banks right.
- **Yaw / Ground Steering**: Q yaws or steers left; E yaws or steers right.
- **Throttle & Boost**: Shift or Plus increases throttle (holding past ninety percent engages War Emergency Power overdrive); Ctrl or Minus decreases throttle; Mouse Wheel adjusts throttle smoothly.
- **Flaps**: G cycles STOL flaps through 0 degrees (cruise/dogfight), 15 degrees (takeoff/tight low-speed turn), and 30 degrees (steep landing approach).
- **Brakes / Airbrake**: B applies wheel brakes on the ground or extends aerodynamic speed-drag in flight for rapid deceleration behind a turning target or on final approach.
- **Primary Weapon (20mm Autocannons)**: Left Mouse Button or Spacebar fires rapid synchronized tracer bursts while held.
- **Secondary Weapon (Surya-2 Missiles)**: Right Mouse Button or X fires a homing missile (tracking the locked hostile when locked, or flying straight if unlocked).
- **Target Lock Cycling**: Tab or T cycles the active radar lock among hostiles and bosses.
- **Countermeasures**: F deploys decoy chaff/flares (short cooldown), breaking incoming enemy aim tracking.
- **Mouse Aim & Camera Look**: Moving the cursor smoothly guides an optional gentle nose-aim assist ring outside a small center deadzone, with keyboard flight input taking immediate override whenever pressed so controls never conflict; dragging with Middle Mouse or Alt plus drag orbits free-look around the plane with smooth auto-recentering.
- **Camera, Audio & Flow**: C toggles Chase and Cockpit view; M toggles mute; Enter confirms launch, retry, or next sortie; Escape opens or returns to Mission Select; 1 through 4 quick-select missions on the title screen.

### Weapons, Ammo, and Damage Model
- **Twin 20mm Autocannons**: 300-round belt per sortie (refillable via runway touch-and-go), rapid fire cadence, fast tracer speed with visible glowing bolts, moderate damage per hit, and barrel heat gauge that rises during sustained fire and cools quickly on release (brief lockout if overheated).
- **Surya-2 Infrared Missiles**: 6 missiles per sortie (mounted visibly under the wings and refillable on touch-and-go), requires holding a target inside the forward seeker cone for roughly one and a half seconds for full lock, deals heavy explosive damage capable of destroying a Kestrel Scout in one hit, crippling a Komodo Gunship, or stripping a major chunk of Ace or Boss health.
- **Player Airframe and Survival**: Player starts each sortie with 100 percent airframe integrity. Enemy fighter gunfire deals light-to-moderate damage per hit with clear visual and audio feedback; boss flak bursts deal heavier splash damage if flown straight through; hard runway bounces or off-runway collisions inflict structural landing damage. Reaching zero hull integrity destroys the aircraft with a dramatic explosion and smoke plume and displays the Shot Down / Crash Incident Report.
- **Runway Touch-and-Go Resupply**: Touching down smoothly on any non-destination friendly airstrip during a sortie restores 30 percent fuel, repairs 25 percent hull, refills the 20mm cannon belt, and reloads up to 4 Surya-2 missiles (usable once per airstrip per sortie), rewarding bold bush-pilot airmanship under fire.

### Enemy AI Behavior and Difficulty Ramp
- **Mission 1 — Nusa Indah Mail Run (Rookie)**: 3 Kestrel Scouts arriving in two forgiving waves over calm seas. Raiders fly predictable sweeping passes with modest firing cadence so new pilots learn aiming, lead pippers, missile lock, courier rings, and smooth STOL landings together.
- **Mission 2 — Gunung Api Medical Express (Intermediate)**: 2 Kestrel Scouts and 2 armored Komodo Gunships contesting the smoky volcano ascent in 7-knot winds and tighter fuel. Gunships take sustained cannon bursts or missile hits and patrol the high-altitude rings aggressively, rewarding use of volcanic updrafts and War Emergency Power.
- **Mission 3 — Karang Reef Island Hopper (Veteran)**: 2 Kestrels, 1 Komodo Gunship, and the elite crimson Ace Elang Merah in a 10-knot crosswind karst slalom. Elang Merah executes sharp break turns, high-speed diving passes, and tighter lead aiming, requiring disciplined turn energy management and timely flare pops.
- **Mission 4 — Nusantara Grand Circuit (Ace)**: Full archipelago blockade featuring 2 Kestrels, 2 Komodo Gunships, and the towering Naga Langit Command Airship Boss patrolling above the central islands. Naga Langit features a three-phase health pool, dual flak turrets firing telegraphed bursting flak clouds, and escalating escort waves at phase thresholds; destroying it unleashes a chained multi-fireball detonation before the player brings the courier cargo home to Pelabuhan Utama.

### Scoring, Win, Lose, and Restart Flow
- **Winning a Sortie**: Clear the mission's combat threats (including any Ace or Boss) and bring the Merpati D-7B safely to a stop on the designated destination airstrip. Passing courier rings along the route awards ring and bullseye bonuses and guides navigation; completing all rings unlocks a route-completion multiplier.
- **Score Breakdown**: Total Sortie Score sums Hostiles Destroyed (400 per Kestrel Scout, 750 per Komodo Gunship), Ace or Boss Bounty (1,500 for Elang Merah, 2,500 for Naga Langit), Courier Rings and Bullseyes, Par Time Bonus, Touchdown Landing Grade Bonus (Butter, Smooth, Firm, Hard), Remaining Fuel Bonus, and Surviving Airframe Integrity Bonus, awarding an S, A, B, or C rank badge.
- **Losing a Sortie**: Running airframe integrity to zero via enemy fire, flak, terrain collision, water ditching, or catastrophic hard landing ends the sortie in a dramatic explosion/smoke crash state and presents the Incident Report explaining the exact cause of loss (for example, shot down by raiders, flak impact, water ditching, or excessive sink rate).
- **Instant Restart and Progression**: From any debrief or crash screen, one click or pressing Enter immediately advances to the next sortie or relaunches the current mission cleanly on the departure strip with full state reset, and Routes or Escape returns to the four-mission selector.
